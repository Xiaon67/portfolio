Introduction

Not a traditional path.

I’m KhronosAion — an independent developer exploring artificial intelligence, systems, and modern software. There isn’t a conventional computer-science story behind my work: no formal CS degree, no list of certifications, and no pre-paved roadmap.

Instead, there is curiosity.

  • The curiosity to understand how things work.
  • The curiosity to build something just because it might be possible.
  • The curiosity to take an idea apart, rebuild it, break it again, and learn something from the process.

This website is not meant to prove expertise.
It is a record of exploration.
